UCL Linguistics Short Courses

We are a short training programme, organised by UCL Linguistics PhD students, introducing students of all levels to a broad range of linguistics topics.

Courses are free to attend and open to everyone. Whether you're accompanying your studies or just looking to dive deeper into these fascinating areas, you're welcome.
